
* Point system:  
  * **General Events** — most frequent; attendance should be encouraged  
  * **GBMs** — more important than General Events  
  * **External Events** — most impactful and least frequent  
    **Proposed Scholarship System**  
    **Base Points**  
* General Event \= **5 points**  
* GBM \= **10 points**  
* External Event( regionals/nationals) \= **15 points**

  **General Event Attendance Bonuses**

* Attend at least **50%**\+ of General Events \= **\+10 points**  
* Attend at least **75%+** of General Events \= **\+20 points**  
* Attend **95-100%** of General Events \= **\+30 points**  
* **Tiebreaker** \- first person to reach the threshold

Active member \= attended 2 gbms \+ 3 events
